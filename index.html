About St. Theresa Parish

Welcome to St. Theresa of the Child Jesus, Malava Parish! Our parish community is dedicated to faith, love, and service through the teachings of the Catholic Church. We strive to grow spiritually together by worship, prayer, and outreach to our neighborhood.
