About Our Parish

St. Therese of Lisieux Malava Catholic Parish, founded in 190,1950 is a welcoming community in Malava, Kenya, dedicated to the Archdiocese of Kakamega, serving families, youth and seniors through worship, education, and charity. Led by our beloved priest, Fr. Vincent Sanga, we serve families, youth, and seniors through various ministries and outreach programs.

Location: Malava, Kakamega County, Kenya
