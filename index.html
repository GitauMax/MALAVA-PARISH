About Our Parish

St. Therese of Lisieux Malava Catholic Parish, founded in 1950, is a welcoming community in the Archdiocese of Kakamega, serving families, youth, and seniors through worship, education, and charity. Led by our beloved priest, Fr. Vincent Sanga, we offer various ministries and outreach programs.

Location: Malava, Kakamega County, Kenya

Contact: 0715959430 | malavaparish@gmail.com
