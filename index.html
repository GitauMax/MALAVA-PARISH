About Our Parish

St. Mary's Catholic Parish, founded in 1950, is a welcoming community dedicated to spreading the Gospel through worship, education, and charity. Led by our beloved pastor, Fr. John Smith, we serve families, youth, and seniors through various ministries and outreach programs.
