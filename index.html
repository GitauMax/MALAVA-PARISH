Welcome to St. Mary's Catholic Parish

About Us

Founded in 1905, St. Mary's Catholic Parish has faithfully served our community for over a century. Our mission is to foster a welcoming and nurturing environment where faith, hope, and love grow.

We invite you to join us in worship, community service, and fellowship to live the teachings of Christ in daily life.
